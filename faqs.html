Student Visa

Typically, you require a valid acceptance letter from an established institution, valid passport, academic transcripts, evidence of complete financial support, and the results of your language proficiency test to apply for a student visa.

The processing time for student visas depends a lot on the country you are applying for. However, it generally takes anywhere between 4 and 8 weeks for the entire process. Our team will provide you with thorough assistance to ensure you don’t experience any delays.
