Privacy Policy

Home Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information SxopeHit collects when you use our recruitment, student visa, immigration and investment services, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Who we are

SxopeHit (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a recruitment, immigration and student visa consultancy with its head office at Office #105, 2nd floor, Chaudhary Center Sodhiwal, Near Yateem Khana, Multan Road, Lahore, Pakistan. You can reach us at support@sxopehit.com or +92 3200557 755.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to assess your eligibility and progress your application. Depending on the service, that may include:

  • Identity and contact details — name, date of birth, passport details, address, phone number and email address.
  • Education and employment history — transcripts, degrees, certificates, experience letters and your CV.
  • Language test results such as IELTS, PTE or Duolingo scores.
  • Financial information — bank statements and proof of funds, where an embassy or institution requires it.
  • Medical and police clearance documents, where a destination country requires them.
  • Records of our correspondence with you, including messages, call notes and appointment bookings.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Assess your eligibility and advise you honestly on which routes are realistic.
  • Prepare, submit and track applications to employers, institutions and immigration authorities.
  • Arrange interviews, medical tests and pre-departure briefings.
  • Respond to your enquiries and keep you updated on the progress of your case.
  • Meet our legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

Who we share it with

We share your information only where it is necessary to deliver the service you have asked for. That may include prospective employers, universities and colleges, embassies and immigration authorities, approved medical centres, and attestation or translation services.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep your file for as long as we are working with you, and afterwards for as long as we are required to for legal, tax or regulatory reasons. When we no longer need it, we delete it or render it anonymous.

Keeping your information secure

We restrict access to your file to the staff handling your case, and we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete information we no longer have a reason to keep.
  • Stop using your information for a particular purpose.

To make a request, email support@sxopehit.com. We may need to verify your identity before we act.

Cookies

Our website uses only what is necessary for the site to function and to remember basic preferences in your browser. We do not use it to build advertising profiles. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Please check back periodically.

Contact us

If you have a question or a complaint about how we handle your information, contact us at support@sxopehit.com, call +92 3200557 755, or write to us at Office #105, 2nd floor, Chaudhary Center Sodhiwal, Near Yateem Khana, Multan Road, Lahore, Pakistan.
