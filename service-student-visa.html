Don’t fall for the fake scams and policies of consultants, mostly in Lahore. Here’s what you usually face and how Sxope Hit Recruitment Services helps you.

Low IELTS Score Preventing University Acceptance

Do not worry about having a low IELTS score. Our study abroad counseling involves guidance about alternative options like proficiency certificates and abroad universities that do not require IELTS at all.

Confusion About Visa Rules

Student visa process and interview preparation require proper planning. We offer expert study visa assistance for students who are eager to study abroad. We advise you accordingly because every country has its own study visa requirements.

Poor Student Profile & Low Bank Statement

A weak profile or poor presentation can ruin your chances even with good grades. Our study abroad consultants guide you on preparing the profile, including the CV, motivational letter, and cover letter, and also instruct about bank statement solutions.

Many students are misled by false promises and unverified agencies. We are trusted, verified overseas education consultants in Lahore with proven results and honest guidance.

How We Work

Our Consultancy Solutions

Here is our step-by-step procedure after you hire us as your study abroad advisor.

01

Profile Assessment

Identify Strengths, Gaps & Opportunities

  • Conduct a detailed academic and background evaluation
  • Analyze previous visa refusals (if any) and identify weak areas
  • Assess IELTS/PTE position and improvement scope
  • Review the financial profile for embassy compliance
  • Create a personalized admission & visa strategy roadmap
  • Provide honest feedback with no false promises
02

University & Scholarship Matching

Maximize Chances Within Your Budget

  • Shortlist universities aligned with your grades and IELTS score
  • Suggest budget-friendly institutions and countries with high visa ratios
  • Identify scholarship and fee waiver opportunities
  • Compare tuition fees, living costs, and ROI
  • Apply strategically to maximize acceptance chances
  • Avoid high-risk universities that trigger visa refusal
03

IELTS & Alternative Strategies

Score Improvement or Pathway Options

  • Provide IELTS band improvement planning
  • Recommend alternative tests (PTE, Duolingo, where applicable)
  • Suggest pathway/foundation programs for low IELTS
  • Guide on countries with flexible English requirements
  • Prepare interview confidence & communication skills
04

SOP & LOR Crafting

Professionally Written to Impress Admissions

  • Conduct a 1-on-1 interview to understand your background
  • Draft a customized SOP (statement of purpose) tailored to your chosen country
  • Address academic gaps or low grades strategically
  • Align SOP with embassy expectations to reduce visa risk
  • Edit and refine LORs (letter of recommendation) for maximum impact
  • Ensure plagiarism-free, authentic documentation
05

Visa & Documentation Guidance

Step-by-Step Embassy Support

  • Complete visa file preparation and checklist verification
  • Guide on GIC, bank statement & financial proof requirements
  • Review and organize all supporting documents
  • Conduct mock embassy interview preparation
  • Double-check application forms to avoid rejection mistakes
  • Track application status and guide until visa decision
06

Post-Offer Support

Travel Planning & Pre-Departure Orientation

  • Assist with accommodation guidance
  • Help with flight booking strategy
  • Provide pre-departure briefing (culture, laws, work rights)
  • Guide on health insurance & airport procedures
  • Connect students with alumni/community abroad
  • Offer ongoing support even after you land
